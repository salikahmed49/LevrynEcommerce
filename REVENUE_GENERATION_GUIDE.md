## 2. **Google AdSense (Display Ads)** 📊

**NOT RECOMMENDED for e-commerce** - Here's why:
- ❌ Distracts customers from buying YOUR products
- ❌ Low earnings (Rs. 50-200 per 1,000 visitors)
- ❌ Looks unprofessional
- ❌ Competitors' ads might show on your site

**Better Alternative:** Focus 100% on selling your products.

## 3. **Affiliate Marketing** 🤝

**Earn commissions by promoting complementary products.**

### **How It Works:**
1. Join affiliate programs
2. Add affiliate links to your site
3. Earn commission when customers buy through your link

### **Recommended Programs:**

**A. Daraz Affiliate Program**
- Commission: 2-8% on sales
- Products: Beauty accessories, gift boxes, etc.
- Link: [Daraz Affiliate](https://www.daraz.pk/affiliate/)

**B. Amazon Associates (International)**
- Commission: 3-10%
- Products: Perfume accessories, atomizers, etc.

### **Implementation:**
```
Blog Section Ideas:
- "Top 10 Perfume Storage Tips" (link to storage boxes)
- "How to Make Perfume Last Longer" (link to moisturizers)
- "Best Gift Ideas for Perfume Lovers" (link to gift sets)

Potential Earnings: Rs. 5,000-15,000/month
```
